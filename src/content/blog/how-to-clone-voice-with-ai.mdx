## Phase 1: Environmental Setup

The AI is a mirror; give it clear audio, and it will give you a perfect clone.
*   **Quiet Room**: Avoid rooms with echo.
*   **Proximity**: Keep your phone **6-8 inches** from your mouth.
*   **Microphone**: Your phone's built-in mic is perfect when used correctly.

## Phase 3: The Golden Sample

This is the most important step. Record for **15-60 seconds.**
> **Expert Tip**: Don't read like a robot. Speak as if you're telling a story to a friend. The AI needs to hear your natural cadence, breath rhythm, and authentic inflection. Read our [natural vs robotic AI voice guide](/blog/natural-vs-robotic-ai-voices) for more details.

---

## Phase 4: Neural Training

Once you submit, our zero-shot acoustic model analyzes your unique vocal timbre. This structural analysis takes **under 15 seconds.** You can immediately type text and generate new audio.

---

## Phase 5: Directing Your AI

To make your clone sound truly human, use punctuation as a director:
*   **Commas**: Natural breath pauses.
*   **Ellipses (...)**: Thoughtful hesitations.
*   **Exclamations**: Increased energy and vocal projection.
