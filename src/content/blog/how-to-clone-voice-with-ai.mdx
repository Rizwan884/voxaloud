## Phase 1: Environmental Optimization

The AI is a mirror; if you give it garbage audio, it will give you a garbage clone. Before opening the **AI audio generator**, you must optimize your recording environment.

### The "Soft Room" Rule
Find a room with carpets, curtains, and pillows. These absorb sound reflections (echo) which confuse the AI model. 

### Critical Factors:
*   **Background Noise**: Turn off fans, AC units, and computers. Even a subtle hum can degrade a **realistic AI voice**.
*   **Proximity**: Keep your phone **6-8 inches** from your mouth. 
    *   *Too close*: You'll get "plosives" (popping P sounds).
    *   *Too far*: You'll get room reverb and echo.

## Phase 3: Capturing the Reference Audio

This is the heart of **how to clone voice with AI**. In the app, select **"New Voice."** You will be prompted to record a sample.

### The Golden Sample
Aim for **60-90 seconds** of continuous speech. 
*   **Don't** use your "reading voice." 
*   **Do** speak as if you are talking to a friend. 
*   **Tip**: Tell a story about your childhood or explain your favorite hobby. 

The AI needs to hear your natural inflections—the way your voice goes up when you're excited and down when you're thoughtful. This variety is what makes a **realistic AI voice** truly sing.
