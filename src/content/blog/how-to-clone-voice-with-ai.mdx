## Phase 1: Environmental Setup

The AI is a mirror; give it clear audio, and it will give you a perfect clone.
*   **Quiet Room**: Avoid rooms with echo.
*   **Proximity**: Keep your phone **6-8 inches** from your mouth.
*   **Microphone**: Your phone's built-in mic is perfect when used correctly.

---

## Phase 2: Get the Engine

Search for **"FishVoice"** on the App Store or Play Store. It's natively optimized for mobile, meaning it uses your phone's hardware to its full potential.

---

## Phase 3: The Golden Sample

This is the most important step. Record for **60-90 seconds.**
> **Expert Tip**: Don't read like a robot. Speak as if you're telling a story to a friend. The AI needs to hear your natural excitement and tone.

---

## Phase 4: Neural Training

Once you submit, our cloud servers analyze your vocal chords' unique timbre. This structural analysis takes **under 5 minutes.** You'll get a notification when your digital twin is ready.

---

## Phase 5: Directing Your AI

To make your clone sound truly human, use punctuation as a director:
*   **Commas**: Natural breath pauses.
*   **Ellipses (...)**: Thoughtful hesitations.
*   **Exclamations**: Increased energy.
