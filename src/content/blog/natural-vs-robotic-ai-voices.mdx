## 1. The Concatenative Era vs. Neural Modeling

Older systems relied on concatenative synthesis, which sliced up recorded voice samples and stitched them together. The resulting audio sounded disjointed and mechanical. Today, a modern **neural voice generator** utilizes deep learning to predict the acoustic patterns of a sentence in real-time, matching standard human inflections and breaths.

This transition is why modern voice synthesis feels authentic and engaging rather than repetitive.

---

## 2. Key Attributes of Realistic TTS

High-fidelity neural synthesis stands out due to several technical features:
*   **Intonation Modeling:** Pitch raises on questions and pauses on commas.
*   **Phoneme Accuracy:** Perfect pronunciation across specialized terminology.
*   **Timbre Richness:** Warm, full-sounding audio output (up to 44kHz).
