## The Evolution of Voice

To understand the breakthrough of the **FishVoice app**, we must look back at synthetic speech history. For decades, Text-to-Speech was defined by robotic, choppy sounds known as "Concatenative Synthesis." 

Today, we use **Neural Text to Speech (NTTS)**. By training massive neural networks, AI finally learned the subtle nuances of human speech:
*   **Breath Patterns**: Natural pauses between thoughts.
*   **Prosody**: The musical rhythm of a sentence.
*   **Emotion**: The difference between a question and a command.

---

## Defining AI Voice Cloning

AI voice cloning is the process of using deep learning models to generate a synthetic replica of a human voice. Unlike standard TTS, cloning takes your **vocal fingerprint** and builds a generative model.

> "A voice clone is more than a recording; it is a generative model that understands the logic of how a specific person speaks."

---

## The Science of Sound

The **FishVoice engine** handles the complexity in three key stages:

### 1. Feature Extraction
The AI takes your reference audio and extracts **mathematical embeddings** that represent your pitch and timbre.

### 2. Spectrogram Generation
The model predicts a **Mel-spectrogram**—a visual blueprint of your voice frequencies.

### 3. High-Res Vocoding
A specialized neural network translates that blueprint back into clear, audible sound waves.
