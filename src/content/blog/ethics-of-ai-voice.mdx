With great power comes great responsibility. As **AI Voice Cloning** becomes more accessible, the creative community is facing new ethical questions about identity, consent, and truth in media.

### Understanding the Risks
The primary concern with voice cloning is the creation of "Deepfakes"—audio that sounds like a specific person saying things they never actually said. This can be used for misinformation, fraud, or character assassination.

### Our Commitment to Safety
At Fish Audio Online, we believe in the positive power of AI. Our mission is to democratize vocal expression, not to facilitate deception. That's why we emphasize:
- **Commercial Usage Rights:** We ensure that our users have the legal right to use the audio they generate.
- **Creator Empowerment:** Our tools are designed to help creators find their *own* unique digital voice, rather than just copying others.

### Best Practices for Ethical Creators
1. **Always Get Consent:** Never clone a real person's voice without their explicit permission.
2. **Be Transparent:** If you are using AI voices in a way that might be mistaken for a specific human, include a disclaimer.
3. **Focus on Originality:** Use our [AI Voice Generator](/ai-voice-generator) to explore unique characters and styles that don't rely on existing identities.

The future of AI is bright, but it requires a foundation of trust. By following ethical guidelines, we can ensure that neural speech synthesis remains a tool for innovation and creativity.
