## 2. Best Practices for High-Quality Cloning

To get the most natural output, follow these recording guidelines:
*   **Clear Recording:** Use a high-quality microphone and record in a quiet room with no echo.
*   **Vocal Range:** Speak naturally and include a range of normal pitch and expression.
*   **Clean Audio:** Remove background hiss or breath noise before uploading.
